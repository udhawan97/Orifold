	You imported a scan or a photo of a document and ⌘F finds nothing on the page.
</Callout>

## Choose what Orifold should recognize

1. Open **Inspector → OCR**.
2. Under **Pages**, keep **Scans only** for the normal safe path. Orifold targets pages that
   do not already have useful searchable text.
3. Choose **All visible** only when an existing text layer is wrong or incomplete. Orifold
   warns you before this broader pass.
4. Leave **Language** on **Automatic**, or choose a specific language when you know the scan's
   language and want Vision to use it directly.
5. Keep **Continue after a page fails** on when a useful partial result is better than stopping
   the entire document. Turn it off when every page must succeed together.
6. Click **Make Searchable**. Progress and Cancel stay visible while Orifold works.

<Callout variant="tip">
	OCR runs locally using Apple's Vision framework. A scan of your passport, a lease, or a
	medical form never leaves the machine.
</Callout>

## Read the quality receipt

When the run finishes, the Inspector shows a receipt rather than silently declaring success.
It records how many pages were requested and recognized, any skipped page numbers, recognized
and low-confidence line counts, average confidence, and whether changed documents passed the
structural postflight.

Review the highlighted pages when the receipt flags skipped pages or low-confidence text. A
successful structural check means the updated PDF is readable; it does not mean every word in a
difficult scan was recognized perfectly.

## What you should see

⌘F now finds recognized text on the page. Orifold adds a searchable layer to the original page
instead of rebuilding it, preserving page geometry and existing annotations. The operation is
also available to Undo and Redo like other workspace changes.

<Callout variant="note">
	Run OCR before editing page text or objects. If the workspace already contains committed text
	or object edits, undo those edits before OCR; Orifold blocks the operation so one page-rewrite
	path cannot overwrite another.
</Callout>
