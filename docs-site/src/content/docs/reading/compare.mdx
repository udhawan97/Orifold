<Callout variant="whentouse">
	You have two versions of a document and need to find changed pages, visible regions, or wording.
</Callout>

## Start a comparison

1. Open the newer or working draft in Orifold.
2. Choose **File → Compare With…** or **More → Compare With…**.
3. Pick the other PDF.

The comparison workspace pairs pages side by side. Changed regions are highlighted on both
pages, and word-level additions and removals are counted. Use the changed-pages strip to jump
between differences.

## Align drafts whose pages shifted

Use the page offset control when a cover sheet or inserted section moved every later page. The
offset changes page pairing without changing either document. Extra pages on either side remain
visible as one-sided differences.

<Callout variant="note">
	Comparison renders and diffs on your Mac. It respects displayed crop boxes and rotations and
	reads text from preserved source bytes. Password-protected comparison files are declined rather
	than guessed at or partially compared.
</Callout>
