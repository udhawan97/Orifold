## Reset a form

Choose Reset Form to clear every field back to blank.

## Lock answers at export

When exporting, choose to lock form answers. This is intended to convert supported field appearances into page content. It does not prevent a PDF editor from changing document content.

<Callout variant="tip">
	Flattening changes the representation of form controls. Verify the saved answers before sharing; use a separate signature workflow when you need evidence of later changes.
</Callout>

## What you should see

Supported answers appear as page content when you reopen the saved copy. Check every value; a visible or flattened appearance is not proof of correct serialization.

## Verify the saved answers

Form-answer serialization from Reader Mode is still under investigation. Keep the original
and reopen the exported copy in another viewer to verify all answers. A locked appearance
alone does not establish that the correct answer was saved.
