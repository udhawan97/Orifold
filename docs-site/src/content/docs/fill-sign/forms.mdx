## Steps

1. Open the PDF; Orifold detects its form fields automatically.
2. Click a field and type your answer, or check a checkbox.
3. Use the inspector to jump between fields and confirm nothing's missed.

## What you should see

Supported fields show your answers as you type. A visible answer is not proof that it has been serialized into the saved file.

## Current limits

Form-answer serialization from Reader Mode remains under investigation. Keep the original,
save a copy, close it, and reopen it in another PDF viewer to verify every answer before
submitting important paperwork. Locking or flattening a form is not a substitute for this check.

Moving pages between source documents refuses an affected member that contains form widgets
or an AcroForm catalog, before changing the workspace. This conservative refusal includes
forms that are not visible on the selected page. Reordering within one source remains separate.
